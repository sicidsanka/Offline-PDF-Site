Delete your data

Everything is kept on your phone only, so we don’t hold anything to delete. To remove it, go to your phone’s Settings › Apps › Offline PDF › Storage › Clear data. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

The scanner doesn’t open

The scanner is part of Google Play services. Make sure Google Play services is up to date in the Play Store, then try again. The first time, your phone may need a moment to get the scanner ready.

Common questions

Where are my files?

On the Files tab in the app. To keep a copy elsewhere, open the file and choose “Save to device”.

I deleted a file by mistake

Open Settings › Recently deleted. Files stay there for 30 days.

Can I open password-protected PDFs?

Not yet. You can still share them or save them to your device.

Does the app use the internet?

No. It has no internet permission. Links like this website open in your browser, outside the app.
