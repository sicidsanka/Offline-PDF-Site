Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Offline PDF (“the app”), made by Horyaal Tech, handles your information.

The short version

The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. It has no internet permission. Your documents and settings stay on your phone.

Your documents

Documents you scan, import or create are saved only inside the app’s storage on your device. We cannot see them, and they are never uploaded. They are removed if you delete them (after 30 days in Recently deleted), clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app.

No permissions

The app asks for no permissions. It has no internet access and no access to your photos, files or storage. When you choose photos or a PDF, or save a file, you pick it yourself using Android’s own file picker, and the app only gets access to the items you chose.

Scanner

The document scanner is provided by Google Play services on your phone (Google ML Kit). Scanning happens on your device and the scanned images are handed straight back to the app. The app does not send them anywhere. Google Play services is part of your phone and is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

No accounts, ads or analytics

The app has no sign-in, shows no ads, and does not use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.

Sharing and printing

When you share, print or “open with” a file, it goes only to the app or printer you choose. That app’s own privacy policy then applies.

Backups

The app’s data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer, so your documents are not copied to any cloud by the app.

Settings

Your settings (such as theme and file names) and, for free tools with a daily limit, a count of how many times you used each tool today are stored only on your device.

Purchases

If an optional Pro upgrade is added, it will be sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app would only receive a confirmation from Google Play that you own Pro. We will update this policy before that is released.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything from your phone, uninstall the app, or go to Settings › Apps › Offline PDF › Storage › Clear data.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the change is released.
