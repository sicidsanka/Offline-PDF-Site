Questions

Is Offline PDF free?

Yes. Scanning, reading, sharing, printing, Photos to PDF and PDF to image are always free and unlimited. A one-time Pro upgrade may come later for extra tools, bought through Google Play.

Does it need the internet?

No. The app has no internet permission, so it can't send your documents anywhere, even if it wanted to.

Where are my files kept?

Inside the app on your phone. You can save a copy anywhere you like with “Save to device”.

Can I open password-protected PDFs?

Not yet. You can still share them or save them to your device.

I deleted a file by mistake

Deleted files stay in Recently deleted for 30 days, so you can bring them back.
